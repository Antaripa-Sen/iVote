# 🗳️ iVote - Your Vote. Your Democracy.

**Live Website:** https://ivote-c2a54.web.app

---

## 📌 About iVote

iVote is an AI-powered election guide that helps Indian citizens understand the entire voting process — from voter registration to government formation. It combines official election information with an intelligent AI assistant that answers any voting-related question in real-time.

---

## ✨ What iVote Can Do

### 1. 🤖 AI-Powered Election Assistant
- Ask any question about voting rules, registration, or rights
- Get instant answers powered by Groq AI (LLaMA 3.1)
- Examples: "How do I get a voter ID?" "What if my name is missing from the voter list?"

### 2. 📅 Complete Election Timeline
Covers all 6 phases of Indian elections:
- Notification → Nomination → Campaign → Voting → Counting → Government Formation
- Click any phase to get AI-powered detailed explanation

### 3. ✅ Voter Readiness Checklist
Step-by-step interactive checklist:
- Verify EPIC (Voter ID card)
- Check registration status
- Find your polling station
- Know what ID to bring
- Understand EVM voting process

### 4. 🧠 Election Knowledge Quiz
Test your understanding with real election rules:
- Multiple choice questions
- Score tracking
- Learn as you play

### 5. 🌍 India vs USA Comparison
Side-by-side comparison of:
- Political systems
- Election frequency
- Voting methods
- Registration processes

### 6. 📰 Live Political News Feed
Real-time news updates from India's political landscape

### 7. 📊 Key Statistics Display
- 970M+ Registered voters
- 7 Voting phases
- EVM technology information
